Before anything else: the 60-day clock

If you're a returning Filipino resident, your household goods have to arrive within 60 days of the day you land to get duty-free entry. Ship early, ideally before you fly.

Do I need a customs broker?

Not by law. If your name is on the bill of lading as consignee, you can file the declaration yourself. Household goods usually go through the simpler informal entry process.

A broker can still save you days of going from window to window. Get any quote itemized first: most of a big number is usually duties, storage and port fees, not the broker's own fee.
