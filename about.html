About

Written by a family who just did this.

We moved home from North Carolina in 2026. Our container arrived months after we did, we missed the 60-day window, and a broker quoted us ₱500,000. We built this guide so the next family knows what we didn't.

Our promise

  • It's free, and no company pays to be listed or rated.
  • We link to the official rules, and say clearly when something is our estimate.
  • We update the guide when the rules change.
