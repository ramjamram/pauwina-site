Chapter 6

The last mile home

Customs is paid. Now get the container out of the port, to your door, and the empty box back where it belongs.

At the terminal

  1. Pay port handling (arrastre) and any storage at the terminal's billing window.
  2. Get the gate pass. You'll need the customs release, the delivery order and your receipts.
  3. Check the container is ready before your truck goes. Otherwise you pay for a wasted trip.

Booking the truck

  • Use a trucker accredited to enter that terminal. Ask before you book.
  • Check the street can fit a 20-foot container truck, and ask the barangay or subdivision about entry hours.
  • Agree on how many hours you get to unload. Extra waiting time is often charged.
  • Going outside Metro Manila, to Palawan, Cebu, Mindanao? Plan an inter-island leg, or unpack in Manila and send the goods on.

Unloading day

Photograph the seal number before it's cut. Then photograph the doors opening and any damage. Check items off your list as they come out.

Return the empty, get your deposit

Return the container clean and empty to the depot the shipping line names, using their return reference number. Late returns are charged by the day and come out of your deposit.
