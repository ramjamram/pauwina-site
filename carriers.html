Who you hire

The forwarder

Picks up from your house, books the ship, and issues your paperwork. This is the choice that matters most, and the one you actually make.
Whose ship it's on

The ocean carrier

MSC, Maersk, CMA CGM and others. You meet them in Manila, when you pay their local charges and collect the delivery order.

Before you hire a forwarder

  1. Check it's on DTI's list of accredited sea-freight forwarders.
  2. Ask which carrier and which US port, and the real sailing date.
  3. Ask who its Manila agent is, and get their contacts.
  4. Get a written quote that says what is not included at destination.
  5. Ask for express or telex release.

Rules coming on local charges

A draft order from DOF, DOTr and DTI (July 2026) would require container deposits to be refunded within 15 days of the empty container's return. It would also stop detention charges when the delay is the shipping line's fault. It isn't final yet; we'll update this when it is.
